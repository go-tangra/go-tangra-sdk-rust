## Your own notes

Open the note and choose **Remove**. Removing a note cannot be undone.

## Other people's notes

Removing someone else's note needs the `notes:admin` permission, which the
*Notes moderator* role and the built-in *owner* and *admin* roles hold.
Without it, **Remove** answers *You are not allowed to do that*.

Notes of other organisations are never visible, so they cannot be removed
either.

See the [reference](../reference.md#roles) for every role.
