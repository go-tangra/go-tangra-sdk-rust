**Notes** keeps short text notes for your organisation. Everyone with access
to Notes in your tenant sees the same list; nobody outside your tenant does.

## Concepts

- **Note** — a title (up to 200 characters) and an optional body (up to
  10,000 characters), with its author and when it was written.
- **Author** — the person who wrote a note. Authors can remove their own
  notes.
- **Moderator** — someone allowed to remove anyone's notes in the tenant.
